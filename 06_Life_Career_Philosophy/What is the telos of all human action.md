# What is the telos of all human action

> [!abstract] Conceptual Context
> **Category**: [[00_MOCs/Life_Career_and_Philosophy_MOC|Life, Career & Philosophy]]
> **Thematic Constellations**: [[00_MOCs/Hub_Urban_Alienation_and_Identity|Urban Alienation and Identity]]

## Content

- All human action aims at some good
  - Greatest good:
	- Happiness (eudaimonia)
	- ‘Flourishing’
	- Is end itself
	- That for the sake of which everything else is done
	- The final (ultimate) telos of humans
	- Of intrinsic value

## How are they connected?
	- By the very nature of ‘happiness’
	- Happiness is a lifelong activity in accordance with reason & virtue

## How do we become virtuous?
- Virtues (character traits) are instilled through socialization & habituation

## How do we know what is virtuous?
- Only the practical wise person (phronimos) knows

## Aristotle: Levels of Life(Psyche)
## Humans
Reason & deliberation
- Rational life

## All Animals & including humans
Sensation & perception
- Living a life of sensuous
- pleasure seeking & pain avoidance

## Plants & all organic life
- Vegetative
Nutrition, growth
& reproduction
- Pathological state:
loss of reason & sensation
eg. coma

## aretē: virtue, excellence
- 2 types of virtue:
	1. Moral virtue or excellence
	1. Intellectual virtue or excellence

## Wisdom:
Sophia: wisdom in a wider sense, including contemplation
Phronesis: practical wisdom

## ethos: Character

## Theory of the Mean
Virtue lies between...
Deficiency < Virtue < Excess
Too little < The Mean < Too much
Cowardice < Courage < Recklessness
