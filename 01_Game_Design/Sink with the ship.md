## Content

Lightnings surges in the city
charging up the water turn it into a new liquid
It floats and blobs pass thru objects
Soaking any material from the inside
The new liquid scans organic matters

This person is scared
trying to rationalize the situation
in the same time relating his past to his fears
All his knowledge mixes with imagination
His confidence becomes his weakness
He tries to factor the unknown
Foreshadowing in his mind
Narrowing

Budgets give more choices and
Unused budgets becomes disadvantages

Toy story coop game
Obstacles where u need to switch batteries or carry parts
