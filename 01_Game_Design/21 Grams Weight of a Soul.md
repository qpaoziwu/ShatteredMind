## Content

You are the god of death disguised as adventurer’s guild master. The dark-lord’s invasion and the medieval knowledge of people have caused a lot of premature deaths and devalued the souls you collect. You decide to take matters into your own hands by guiding the village as the guild master.

1. Survive the invasion of the dark-lord
2. Dead villagers return souls to you
3. Souls are needed to reproduce villagers
4. Stronger villagers will return more souls

Defeating the boss in the level and a final score will be displayed. Scores are earned through:

1. Souls Collected
2. Monsters Defeated
3. Time elapsed

Like every greek mythology, there are dramas between gods. You, as the bringer of souls, can harvest for other gods to gain their bonuses, or even bargain shady deals with the darklord.

  As a god, you do not control the villagers, but suggest directions for them by posting quests.
  During daytime, villagers will be on their routine and venture out.
  During nighttime, you can make trades with other gods to influence the daytime events.
  Additionally, you can visit villagers to collect their souls early at night.
