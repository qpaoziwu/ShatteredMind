## Content

9numpad directional control

Game with stats can have different servers with different stats variations of the game.

Mini pool, but very heavy table.

Combining 2 lows to get 1 medium, etc.
Combining shares’ disposable stats.

Select Reward, shows cost.
Shows mission value, Select Mission.

Orbs drop from tall places, and picking them up allows you to use its ability.

Bedroom adventure with an orb light puzzle.
Lights off turns the stage into twilight.
Lights on turns the stage back to the bedroom.
Eventually learns to use paper-cutouts to befriend shadows.
Shadows can be safe.
Too bright can blind others.
Shape the light for others.

Don’t Starve+ Concrete Genie.

Pool 8 Balls, but Balls have roles.

Count down games, count up games, balance games, overflow games, five-senses games
redistribution games, distribution games, rollup games

hollow cubes colored when it passes thru

sphere cast and store an int in obj,
Add delay to relative to the int (higher=longer)

film/screen starts to burn, eventually burns off the screen, gameover

 when u use a light for too long, it will game over and burn the screen off playing that effect.
- Slide in a film and play the image.
- Layer a few films to combine effects.
- Forced to burn a film to get through a stage

alter framerates to hide info.

鏡面燈

Rate/stat squishing

Cognitive design techniques that allow muscle memories or preferences

wanting to find someone based on their appearance means your glass is either empty or empty but full of urself,
glass full of yourself empties faster than a full glass

attack that scrambles hp bar,
if hp bar lowers to a gap, it stops

furiously texting
