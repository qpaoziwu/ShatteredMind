## Content

All numbers/values/cost are replaced by candies

The types of candies starts at 3 but can be more for experienced players

**Candy Value** is randomized at the start of the game and
**Reveal** one specific value to a player

**Candy Value** caps at 9 and -9

Player draws 5 cards from the candy box on the start of their turn and can play as many cards as they want

All cards has effects to trigger a **values** change;

After everyone’s turn, there will be a **value** change, determined by the current value of all the positive candies vs negative candies, if the positive value is higher, then it will trigger a -2 to all Candy Value; vice versa, it will trigger a +1 to all, if it’s a draw then nothing happens.

Player can melt cards cost to candies, or consume their own candies to pay costs (equal or more in **Candy Value)** to activate its effect; excess **Candy Value** is lost

If player activates a card with negative cost, they will trigger **Sugar Rush**; Gain one candy of the displayed cost AND activate its effect.

Strong cards will always shift to the highest numerical value of the candy as cost

Normal cards requires quantity of candy, not **Candy Value**

On certain actions/turns will trigger a values change; Value change will always be +1/-1

The UI will always tell u if you have the cost to use the cards by a bright/dimmed candy symbol

Player use deduction and memorization to keep track of the value of the candies

Game ends immediately when draw pile depletes

Most value wins; cards that are not melted will not be counted

Last Round discard hand > end game
