## Content

- Clap to share/switch

**Color Switch**
*2 Player Puzzle Game:*
- Player 1 controls up/down
- Player 2 controls left/right
- Clap to switch directions

Different goals and high five to swap

rotating game board

Dec2:
Camera-Help/
- Update 2 Icons (QR code, Tips Icon)

**Steam Train Driver**
- Sequence of actions to start the engine
- Close the train doors and leave the station
- Slow down during residential area to reduce noise level
- Arriving the station
- Opening door

**[[01_Game_Design/Marshmallow Kingdom|Marshmallow Kingdom]]** *(Canonical Note)*
- Tactile squishy brawling, deforming enemies, and soap-rubbing liquid barriers.

**Worms**
- PVP or vs NPC
- Cute Weapons

**Job Simulator**
- Shopkeeper
- Office-Boy

**Pinball Machine**
- Unlock modules and Build your own pinball machine
- Move arms to active flippers
- Standing on Left/Center/Right + Jump/Swipe/Punch to activate modules
- Arm tilt = platform tilt

**Cats n Lasers**
- Use laser to navigate cat to destroy things

**Night in the museum - Escape from the Museum**
- Countdown then freeze pose to avoid being caught

**Surfing Game**
- Riding the waves

**Escape Room**
- Themed escape room
