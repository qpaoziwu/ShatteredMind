## Content

But all innocent has a explosive collar with unknown killer
and cops are in another team not knowing who are the hostage group

Killer’s objective: Get innocent jailed
Innocent’s objective: Survive and disable collar
Cop’s objective: Arrest the killer

Killer pick one hostage to tie on a stage,
Cops solving the puzzle will release the hostage,

Killer plays with the hostages
Hostages find clues to unlock collar

Killer has to collect objects in the scene
and can kill passerby
he can order innocent to collect objects to confuse innocents
Killer killing cops will tip off innocents

Innocent cannot see their own collar timer
and collar explodes when timer ends

Innocent has to find ways to disable collar
and killer will not know if collar is disabled

AI will decide where to build things based off of their most convenient/contact path
