## Content

Horizontal shooting hide n seek monster,
when the gets close screen starts to pixelated and shaky

vertical shooting fish jumps from below and u need to punch them n shoot them

Fish will suck on ur fist unless u get rid of them

boss, dragon, need to poke their eyes to stun them and punch all the scales then to strip their defence then shoot them

pushing a table of objects with two hands
pushing windows to change the view as a tiny human

The world is under a curse by a baddie where they forget everything after they sleep
U need to guide the villagers to uncover the evil scheme
