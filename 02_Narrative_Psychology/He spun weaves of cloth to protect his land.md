different characters sees a different path

We were in the house I showed you on the sea side, but it was abandoned and it had much more floor than it actually has, and I was trying to set a bed, close the windows because it was very windy but they were constantly opening up again.

i had a horrible dream last night. i dreamed we went to a bar together and you left because u didn’t like my bad social skills and embarrassed you. i didn’t know and thought the drummer was holding your hand but he was holding his girlfriend’s hand. A lot of angry emotions came out from me and i threw the drum sticks on the wall on my way out. I went for the lift and as the lift goes down, my vision went blurry. i saw the lift opened and there’s a white wall with a white button. I realized i shouldn’t press it but my body went first before my blurry mind. The wall retreated and some hands came grab my left arm. I managed to pull myself back and closed the door. And i woke up in the dream within the dream. I was still trying to leave and this time i passed by my friends from tst bar and they saw me leaving. And they followed me to the streets, then a fight broke out. i was tackle down by one of the guys and he started biting my fingers and hand when i’m pushing him away from me. I felt my skin was tearing off like chicken skin. I’m not sure what i can do but i forced myself to wake up. i woke up and lying on bed. Tired to move but can’t and i know i’m still sleeping. I tried to move again and i can tilt my head, but every motion i made, the scene would reset and i’m lying on bed again

i had a dream where i had to manage a building last night. it’s weird because the people are not kind and there are monsters waiting outside. people has to constantly move around the building to avoid the monsters. And i have to find new paths to avoid the monsters and make sure different people can go thru the path without starting a fight.
