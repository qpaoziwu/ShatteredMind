## Content

Sleeping on the floor
Looking out from the handle hole

Being dragged and window changes day night cycle

Room of lanes of chains chained to a fish
Tried to run away in this room of Sea loins
Trying to runaway with Hot air balloon going up a mountain and found by rotary club dad
Climbing thru secret tunnel in a cupboard and time rewinding after entering the tunnel, Trying to run away from mom
Time rewinds, background changed to the 80s HK
Animal puppies being sold for cents
Chained in a room with my back attacked by a buzzing tool
Extreme twitching and muscle cramps while fighting to stay alive from pushing away the buzzing tool with one hand
and can only be stopped by being beheaded
