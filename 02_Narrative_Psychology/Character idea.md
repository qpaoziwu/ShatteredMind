## Content

i am the bad guy
what ever i do, people will treat me as one

are you tired from being a bad guy?

i didn’t have a choice, this is what people sees me as
there is no choice, there is only life
i make a choice, then the choice bites back
it is a fact for some people
no matter what they do, their action makes everyone feels bad

maybe you should try a different approach

i don’t see what others see, i can’t find the common ground where i’m happy and everyone else would feel the same
i want to disappear to a place where my choice doesn’t hurt anyone
but it is not possible, i don’t have the right to choose as long as people are angry
everyone is always angry with me
they see me as a selfish individual
think that i don’t contribute or sacrifice for others
they think that i’m always angry, and up to no good
i tried to talk to people and change that
but at the end people simply don’t like me and I don’t know why
i want to love, i want to hear, want to be treated as everyone else
i want to be normal, but when i look back, i’m just a bad guy
i want to rip off the bad piece of me
but the wound turns me into an uglier person
i bleed and feel the pain but all i hear is i’m as hideous as i sound
I never cry when i’m alone because i know i would be even more uglier than i am
i lick my wound and try to move on and alone am i to the people around
i can stay waiting for the scar to sear but there is no time for me to heal

i am a dream-walker, people wonder how do i enjoy sleeping so much. I haven’t had a good sleep since childhood, all the nightmares I encountered kept me away from getting a goodnight sleep. I am constantly struggling falling back to sleep in the middle of the night, afraid that I would return to the same dream. I had no choice but to force myself awake in bed. One night, I turned to my side and looked at the clock on the wall. Its hour hand just ran past 3, and I was disappointed to know that I will have to return to the same scene for at least 3 more hours. If it were 5 o’clock, I could turn on the lights without my parents yelling at me; but no, it was way too early to keep myself awake, and way too late to realize that my heavy eyelids dragged me back to the nightmares. The nightmares that made my blanket wet like towel, and my heartbeat drumming like the thunder.
