# I

## Content

lived in a perfectly privilege family until I was 8.
That year, My dad lost his business. My mom turned abusive. My brother started to bully me.
I was always the brightest kid with the quickest mind but yet the wisest. My closest relatives weren’t kind to us and It didn’t take long for me to realize my family was in crisis.
After a few years, My dad became verbally abusive. My mom left the family. My brother became depressed.
I lost connection to the world and try to pull on the strings of life. At nights in dreams, I often find myself inside a dark hole. I see ropes hanging down from above. Each string parts just enough that I have to let go to reach to another. And if one string snaps, I will fall back to the beginning. In my mind, I know I have been here before, because I never got out. I remember countless more horrible dreams as vivid as a fresh sear.

My life was almost destroyed when I returned from Canada. I was rejected by a newly hired principal and resorted to attend a public school in the worst neighbourhood. I never knew there was another abyss beyond the chaos and lost the firm grip of my sanity. I was terrified of how many more abyss I may fall into and my fragile self struggled to imagine.
Those years turned into a spiral that took years to unwind. Never have I ever, wish I could go back in time.

I don’t know what I don’t know; don’t know i can reach any rope if I’m not alone.
Do I wait in the hole or do I struggle? Do I yell like a mad man, hoping someone falls into the hole? I spent eternal in the hole and now I am the hole.
